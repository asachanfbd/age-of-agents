# Code of Conduct

We want Age of Agents to be a place where people can contribute without harassment or hostility.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile or open a private security advisory on this repository.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
